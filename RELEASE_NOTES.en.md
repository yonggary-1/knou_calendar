## Knou calendar v1.6.4 — Popup scrolling cleanup

### What's new
- **The popup now has a single scroll area.** With many upcoming tasks, the whole popup and the course list used to scroll separately, so the bottom buttons (📝 과제 메모, MyKNOU ↗, 캘린더 ↗) could stay out of sight. Now the connection status, the sync button, and the bottom buttons are always visible, and only the upcoming-task list in the middle scrolls.

### How to update
Extract `knou_calendar_chromium.zip` one level above the folder your browser loads, overwrite, and press reload (↻) on the extensions page. The widget is unchanged (v1.1.2).

### Assets
- `knou_calendar_chromium.zip`: for Chrome, Edge, Whale, and Opera.
- `knou_calendar_firefox.zip`: package for Mozilla signing (Firefox).
- `KnouWidget.js`: the iPad/iPhone Scriptable widget script (v1.1.2).
